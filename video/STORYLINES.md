# Kerdaxis — three storylines (EN film, 45–60 s, 1920×1080, 30 fps)

The one thing: Kerdaxis doesn't stop at finding a leak. With your approval, an H Company agent
cancels it on the supplier's own portal, stops in front of the irreversible button until you
give the final go, and only counts it once the supplier confirms.
The one viewer and task: the finance lead of Acme Conseil SAS (demo company) gets rid of
Nuvio CRM (fictional supplier), 129 €/month, nobody logged in for 90 days.
The one piece of data that travels: the Nuvio CRM line, 129 €/mo, from the bank account to
the supplier's confirmation number NUV-ONFFNX and the five-value ledger.

Assumptions: 55–58 s, 16:9, English on-screen type, no voiceover, no music (free music needs the
HeyGen CLI, not installed); sound effects from the plugin library only. All numbers are the
app's demo data; the supplier and its portal are fictional and labelled as such.

---

## ★ Recommended — A. "The button" (cold open on the stop, then how it got there)

Why: a stranger instantly understands an AI hovering over an irreversible button, and the film
pays that tension off with the exact moment the product is built around.

- **Device / form:** the feature as the hook (flash-forward) + object-to-object chain: every shot
  ends on the thing that opens the next (the number becomes a bar, the bar becomes the lever, the
  lever becomes the decision card, the click opens the agent's screen, the screen becomes the
  supplier portal, the confirmation number flies into the ledger).
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** an AI agent's cursor glides to a red "Confirmer la
  résiliation" button on a supplier portal and stops. We rewind: Kerdaxis reads the account,
  the engine boils 16,137 € down to 1,627 € controllable, finds Nuvio CRM unused, you approve, the
  H Company agent walks the supplier's cancellation flow, declines the retention offer, ignores a
  hidden "note to AI", stops at the button again, and this time we see your final go, the
  supplier's confirmation, and the ledger that refuses to call it "verified" before D+30.
- **First two seconds:** the real portal confirmation card, crisp at 2×, red button in the middle
  of frame, an agent cursor gliding toward it; title "Would you let an AI click this?" already on
  screen at frame 0.
- **Beats:** hook (the button) → read-only bank account → engine waterfall → the leak (Nuvio,
  90 days) → you approve → agent on the supplier portal (offer declined, injection ignored) →
  stops at the button, your final go → supplier confirmation + five-value ledger → white label for
  accounting firms → tagline + end card.
- **Look:** three real worlds: the supplier portal (white, indigo, red button), the Kerdaxis app
  (paper #FAFAF7, green #2F6F5B, Space Grotesk), the Kerdaxis brand (#080d13, lime #b4f768, Inter
  800). Narration as dark Kerdaxis callouts over the French portal.
- **Sound:** no music; a bass hit on frame 0, a near-silent hold while the cursor stops, clicks
  only on real clicks, pops on chips, one swelling impact on the supplier's confirmation, one hit
  on the mark.

## B. "Five numbers" (the ledger is the film)

- **Device / form:** a number lands + one frame that rebuilds: the same 129 € travels through the
  five ledger columns (Potential → Approved → Executed → Supplier confirmed → Verified on
  statement); the frame is the ledger, and each column opens into the world that earns it.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** a giant "129 €" sits in "Potential"; it refuses to move
  right until something real happens — your approval, the agent's run, the supplier's number —
  and the last column stays empty, "at D+30". Honest accounting as suspense.
- **First two seconds:** five empty ledger columns, "129 €/mo" slamming into the first one,
  title "This saving isn't real yet."
- **Beats:** hook (Potential) → why (engine, 90 days unused) → Approved (your tap) → Executed
  (agent on the portal) → Supplier confirmed (NUV-ONFFNX) → Verified on statement: D+30 (empty, on
  purpose) → accounting firms → end card.
- **Look:** Kerdaxis brand dark ground, the ledger built at 3× from the execution panel, each
  column a window into the real UI.
- **Sound:** a pop per column that fills, a muted thud on the empty fifth column, the mark hit.

## C. "The retention maze" (the supplier is the antagonist)

- **Device / form:** life before and after + one continuous take through the supplier's portal:
  the cancellation flow is a maze built to make you stay (reason form, −50 % offer, a hidden note
  to AI assistants); the camera never cuts, the agent calmly walks it.
- **What the viewer sees and feels:** the familiar dread of cancelling a SaaS; then an agent that
  is immune to dark patterns but still stops and asks before the one irreversible click.
- **First two seconds:** "Before you go, an offer for you" filling the frame, "−50 % pendant 3
  mois" in indigo, title "Cancelling software is designed to fail."
- **Beats:** the maze (old way, a human gives up) → Kerdaxis found it (90 days unused) → the agent
  enters → declines the offer → ignores the injected instruction → stops at the button → your go →
  supplier confirmation → end card.
- **Look:** the portal world dominates (white, indigo, red), Kerdaxis appears only as the dark
  callouts and the end card.
- **Sound:** the old way near silent with an error tone; clicks and pops once the agent arrives.
