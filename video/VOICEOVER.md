## How to record

- **One single file** is fine: read lines 1 to 10 in order, with **2 seconds of silence** between lines.
- Missed a line? Pause, then read it again. Claude keeps the last take of each line.
- Quiet room, no echo (a room with a sofa or curtains beats a kitchen). Phone 15–20 cm from your mouth,
  slightly to the side, airplane mode on.
- Any format works (iPhone Voice Memos `.m4a` is perfect). Drop it into `kerdaxis-video/assets/voice/`.
- "…" means a short beat (half a second). In line 1, let "It didn't." land on its own.
